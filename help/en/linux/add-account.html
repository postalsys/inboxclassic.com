Add a mail account

Add your first account, or another one, by signing in through your browser or with your address and password.

The first time Inbox Classic starts, it opens Add Mail Account. To add another account later, choose File > New Account.

Gmail, Google Workspace, Outlook.com or Microsoft 365

To add the account through your browser:

  1. Click Sign in with Google or Sign in with Microsoft. Your web browser opens the provider’s sign-in page.
  2. Sign in there, then come back to Inbox Classic. The account is added and its mail starts to arrive.

A faded button is turned off on this computer. Click Why? under it to see the reason.

Any other account

To add the account with its password:

  1. Type your name as others should see it in Display name.
  2. Type your address in E-mail address.
  3. Click Next >. Inbox Classic looks up your provider’s settings, which takes a few seconds. Cancel skips the lookup.
  4. Type your password in Password.
  5. Check the server settings, then click Finish. Inbox Classic signs in and downloads your folders.

If your provider wants an app password instead of your usual one, Inbox Classic says so and offers to open the provider’s instructions.

When no settings are found, the server names are guesses: imap. and smtp. followed by your domain. Compare them with what your provider tells you.

Account name is what the folder list calls the account, such as Work. A new account starts with its provider’s name or its domain. You can change it any time in the account’s properties.

To sign in with a sign-in app of your own, click More sign-in options on the first page and choose it.
