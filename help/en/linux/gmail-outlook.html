Gmail and Outlook.com

Gmail, Outlook.com and their business versions take a browser sign-in or an app password, never your usual password.

Google and Microsoft do not let mail programs use your usual password. You have two ways in.

Sign in through your browser

Click Sign in with Google or Sign in with Microsoft in Add Mail Account. You type your password on Google’s or Microsoft’s own page, so Inbox Classic never sees it. Such an account always uses the provider’s own mail servers.

A Microsoft 365 organization may first have to allow Inbox Classic. Microsoft then shows Need admin approval, and Inbox Classic tells you the address where your administrator approves it.

Sign in with Google is turned off at first. Google lets only the test users of Inbox Classic sign in until it has verified the app. Use an app password, or a sign-in app of your own (see Sign-in apps).

Use an app password

  1. Create an app password in your account’s security settings. On Google it is under Security > 2-Step Verification > App passwords. On Microsoft it is under Security > Advanced security options > App passwords.
  2. In Add Mail Account, type your name and address and click Next >.
  3. Click Use App Password.
  4. Type the app password in Password, then click Finish.

When the sign-in expires

If Google or Microsoft ends the sign-in, Inbox Classic says “Sign-in to … was rejected or the session has expired.” It asks whether to sign in again in the browser. Choose Yes and sign in once more.
