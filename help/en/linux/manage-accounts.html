Change, order and remove accounts

Rename an account, change its settings or password, put your accounts in the order you like, or remove one.

Change an account

  1. Choose File > Accounts. The window lists your accounts.
  2. Select one and click Properties, or double-click it.
  3. Change what you need and click OK.
  • Account name is what the folder list calls the account, such as Gmail or Work.
  • Display name is the name the people you write to see.
  • To keep the password, leave Password empty.
  • An account that signs in through the browser says which sign-in app it uses.

Add in the same window adds another account.

Put your accounts in order

Drag an account in the folder list, or its row under All Inboxes, above or below another. A line shows where it goes. Or right-click the account and choose Move Up or Move Down. The folder list and the rows under All Inboxes both follow the new order.

The account’s menu

Right-click an account in the folder list for these commands:

  • Send and Receive checks that account alone.
  • Mark All Read marks every folder of the account read.
  • New Folder, Properties, Move Up and Move Down.

Remove an account

Removing an account deletes the mail downloaded for it and its saved password from this computer. The mail on the server stays.

To remove an account:

  1. Choose File > Accounts.
  2. Select the account and click Remove, or press Del.
  3. Confirm. The account is removed from the folder list.
